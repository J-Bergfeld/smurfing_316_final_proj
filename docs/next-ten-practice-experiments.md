# Next ten practice experiments

## Control and method

The reported practice champion is `d39531d22fa424d7093dbbdca6ae47ef10a6dd7d` at **5.56**. The user reports **5.19** for `0519eb0`, whose runtime differs from that champion only by reducing the healthy combat cube radius from 70 m to 60 m. `25f4e76`, the pulled `sam` tip, restores the 70 m radius and adds selective approach boost; the user reports **5.27**. Run IDs, exact times and scorer versions are not established here, so score differences are observational and may include sampling or benchmark differences.

The strategy is to make ten **adaptive, independently attributable official tests**, not to bundle ten speculative changes. Start each candidate from the current best verified official build. The tested boost has a lower reported score, so do not silently carry it into new candidates. Freeze the exact source and Docker flags, check the intended trigger with focused tests, run protocol and restricted-container checks, and submit without requiring a lengthy local tournament. Record the submitted revision, configuration, run ID, scorer and result. Keep the best official entry available.

Rank is conviction in the mechanism and usefulness under the deadline, not a claimed probability of an official score increase. The exact order should change if new official feedback or a correctness check contradicts a hypothesis. Use later slots for the strongest revision or a well-motivated combination after testing factors separately.

The opportunistic-reload candidate `af7e509` is enabled by `BOT_OPPORTUNISTIC_RELOAD=1`; `BOT_APPROACH_BOOST=0` restores the 5.56 control's default movement. It requires a sustained 0.4 s no-shot window, a low nonempty magazine, and safety checks for recent damage, nearby threats and the predicted reload window. Its focused tests passed. The restricted-container checks answered 300/300 standard observations and 1,136/1,136 on a longer recorded replay on time. The full suite has one failure and one error already present on the baseline. The user reports an official score of **4.91**, below the reported 5.56 control; the runs were separate, so the change is not proven to have caused the difference.

## Do not repeat as if new

- SAM already has a practice/squad stance, weapon-aware preferred ranges (82 m gun-only, 120 m laser and 28 m lightning in the relevant combat cases), positional field and corner duel. The pressure-policy force-squad and weapon-range toggles are not new SAM features.
- Preserve supply drops off and the 70 m combat-cube control unless testing one explicitly. A 60 m radius has the reported 5.19 result, and the historical supply-drop results were weaker but independently sampled.
- Broad attacker-priority, sound scanning, generalized dodge, aggressive retreat bundles, wholesale roof/pressure policy and reload/laser-timing bundles have prior negative or confounded evidence. A genuinely different scoped fix may be worthwhile; do not attribute the older result to one bundled factor.
- The candidate gun near-surface clearance screen had one −10 and two tied paired proxy games; the combat-unstuck guard had four tied pairs and one late candidate action. These small local samples do not prove official harm, but given the deadline they are not first choices.
- Local placement or reference-action agreement is not official performance. A high-conviction hypothesis still needs correct implementation, a sensible failure case, and official scoring.
